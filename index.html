How does it work?

  1. A new set every dayEvery day (Asia/Kolkata date), the next 10 + 10 + 10 questions from the bank. When the bank is exhausted, the set cycles again.
  2. Answers at the endCorrect/incorrect answers are not shown during the test. On submitting, you will get your score, accuracy, time taken and an explanation for every question.
  3. Palette and timerUse the palette to go to any question and mark questions for review. Your answer is saved as soon as you select it. The daily test has 45 minutes; when time runs out, the test submits automatically.
